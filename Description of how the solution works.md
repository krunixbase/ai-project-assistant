Purpose of the Solution
The provided solution was designed as an assistant supporting project and business conversations. Its purpose is to help organize the meeting context and prepare suggested questions or answers during the conversation—without interfering with its flow.

How it works
The solution operates as a tool launched in parallel with the meeting (e.g., online conversations), without the need for installation on a work computer and without any visible intervention on the other party's side.

Before starting the conversation, the user can:

provide the meeting context (e.g., topic, conversation stage, prior arrangements),

define the purpose of the conversation or areas they want to focus on.

During the conversation:

the solution analyzes the previously provided context,

the user independently initiates the generation of suggestions (e.g., questions or answers),

the assistant does not operate autonomously and does not generate content without explicit user action.

Control and Security
The solution was designed to:

not cause delays in the conversation,

not interfere with its natural flow,

leave full control to the user.

The assistant does not speak independently and does not make decisions without the user's initiative.

Possibility for Further Development
The provided mechanism provides a coherent foundation for further development of functionalities, such as:

adapting the suggestion style to user preferences,

expanding the conversation context logic,

expanding the range of supported meeting scenarios.

The scope of further work can be determined in stages, depending on needs and the results of verification of the current solution.
