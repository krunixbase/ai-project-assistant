IInstructions for verifying the delivered solution

The delivered solution demonstrates a working assistant mechanism supporting project conversations, in accordance with the established scope.

Verification involves:

Reading the solution's description.

Checking how the conversation is contextualized before it begins.

Assessing the user's suggestion mechanism (responses only after clicking).

Verifying that the solution runs parallel to the meeting and does not autonomously interfere with the conversation.

The solution does not require installation on a work computer and has been designed to allow for further development of its functionality as needed.

If you have any questions regarding its operation or expansion options, please feel free to contact me.
